# HLF-BRIEF-01 — HarbourLight Foods Company Brief

## Scenario status

HarbourLight Foods Pte Ltd is a fictional Singapore company created for C1311 classroom practice. Any resemblance to an actual organisation is coincidental.

## Entity and operations

- Legal name: HarbourLight Foods Pte Ltd
- Reporting period for the exercise: 1 January to 31 December 2025
- Comparison period: 1 January to 31 December 2024
- Included operations: the Jurong manufacturing site and Woodlands distribution site
- Corporate activities included: Singapore management and reporting functions
- Activities outside Singapore: none in this synthetic source pack
- Product: shelf-stable packaged food supplied to retailers and food-service customers

## Reporting intent

Management wants a concise FY2025 sustainability-reporting pack that can support internal decisions and future external reporting. The pack should explore impact-focused and investor-focused reporting lenses without presuming that any jurisdictional rule applies to this fictional entity.

Primary intended readers are management, customers, investors and other affected stakeholders. The reporting steering group consists of the Chief Financial Officer, Operations Director, Human Resources Director and Sustainability Lead.

## Available evidence

- Site electricity, natural-gas, diesel, water, waste, production and worker-hour records for FY2024 and FY2025
- Corporate revenue for both years
- Synthetic stakeholder and business-risk notes
- A deliberately flawed prior-year disclosure for critique
- No externally verified greenhouse-gas inventory
- No approved Scope 3 inventory
- No approved public greenhouse-gas target
- No independent conclusion on framework conformity

## Governance facts

The reporting steering group meets quarterly. Metric owners submit source records to the Sustainability Lead. The source pack does not show an approved factor-selection policy, formal framework-applicability decision or independent review of the complete sustainability information.

## Required caution

The course factors are synthetic training values. A real reporting team must obtain current approved factors, confirm entity and operational boundaries, establish jurisdictional applicability and follow its organisation's governance process before publication.
