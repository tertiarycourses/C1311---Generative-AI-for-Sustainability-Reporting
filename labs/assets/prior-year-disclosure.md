# HLF-PRIOR-01 — Deliberately Flawed Prior-Year Working Text

> Training note: This paragraph intentionally contains unsupported and inconsistent claims. It must not be copied into a final report.

HarbourLight Foods achieved industry-leading environmental performance in 2024. All company emissions fell by 20% because of our successful equipment-upgrade programme, and our operations had zero significant environmental impacts. Total carbon emissions were 450 tonnes, including all value-chain emissions, and the company fully aligned with every major sustainability framework. Our energy-efficiency programme will guarantee a further 50% reduction by 2030. These results were independently verified and demonstrate that HarbourLight has eliminated its material climate risks.

## Known review prompts

- Is the reporting boundary stated?
- Does the source pack support the total, percentage change, cause, target or verification claim?
- Does the text distinguish Scope 1, Scope 2 and Scope 3?
- Is any framework statement tied to an exact requirement and applicability decision?
- Does the wording present a balanced account of uncertainty and limitations?
