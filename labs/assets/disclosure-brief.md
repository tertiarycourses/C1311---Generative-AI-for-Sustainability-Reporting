# HLF-DISC-BRIEF — FY2025 Energy and Emissions Disclosure Brief

## Intended output

- One 300–400 word reader-facing energy-and-emissions disclosure
- One 100–130 word executive summary derived only from the approved disclosure
- One claim ledger covering every material statement

## Required content

1. Entity, period and included sites
2. Method and synthetic-factor limitation
3. FY2025 Scope 1, Scope 2 and combined training results
4. FY2024 comparison and revenue-intensity comparison
5. Balanced observation that does not assign an unsupported cause
6. Governance facts supported by HLF-BRIEF-01
7. Missing Scope 3 inventory, missing approved public target and next evidence priorities

## Tone

Neutral, precise and readable. Use short sentences, defined units and visible limitations. Do not add a strategy, target, initiative, causal explanation, external verification or framework statement that is absent from the approved evidence.

## Canonical quantitative source

Use the learner's independently checked Lab 4 workpaper. Expected training totals are supplied only as a classroom control; the retained spreadsheet formulas and source lineage remain authoritative for the exercise.
