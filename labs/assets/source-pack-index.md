## Rules for use

- Preserve the source IDs and raw values.
- Write `MISSING` when the source pack is silent.
- Treat the factor file as synthetic training data only.
- Open the official URLs before retaining any framework or jurisdiction statement.
- Keep AI-generated work in the `review/` area until a human has verified it.
- Do not add real employer, customer, supplier, employee or personal information.
